# Runlens Trust Principles (the Spine)

> Source of truth for every trust-related page, clause, and screen. Everything in
> `docs/trust/` paraphrases these. Edit here first, then propagate.
>
> Status: DRAFT — not reviewed by counsel. Nothing in this folder is legal advice.

1. **Plain language, always.** If a runner can't understand it, it doesn't build
   trust. No legalese where a sentence will do.

2. **Your video is yours.** You own your footage and your results. You can see
   them, export them, and delete them — anytime. Deleting a run removes the
   video and every derived artifact from our storage.

3. **We're a coach, not a clinic.** Runlens gives training feedback, not medical
   diagnosis. For pain or injury, we point you to a professional — on purpose.

4. **Honest recommendations.** *(Future — shoe recs not yet shipped.)* When we
   suggest a shoe and earn a commission, we say so at the point of
   recommendation — and commission never changes what we recommend.

5. **We show our work.** We tell you how confident an analysis is and what a
   phone camera can't see. Directional trends, not clinical measurements. No
   black-box overclaiming.

6. **Private by default, honest about the cloud.** We collect the minimum we
   need and never sell your personal data. Analysis runs on our servers today —
   we say so plainly rather than implying on-device processing we don't have.

## Voice & tone

- Knowledgeable running friend, not a law firm or med-tech vendor. (The About
  page already has this voice — match it.)
- Confident but humble on accuracy; humility reads as credibility.
- Specific beats vague: name real retention windows, real subprocessors, real
  controls with real screenshots.
- Trust is a differentiator, not a chore — competitors lean on lab credentials;
  Runlens leans on radical clarity and user control.

## Known truth constraints (don't overclaim)

- Processing is **cloud-based** (Render workers), not on-device. Principle 6 is
  worded accordingly. On-device is a possible future wedge, not a current claim.
- Run detail pages (`/runs/[id]`) are **public by link** by design (sharing).
  Copy must disclose this, not hide it.
- Raw videos are currently retained **until the user deletes the run** — there
  is no automatic retention window yet. Don't state one until it's built.
