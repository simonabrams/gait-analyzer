## 4. Special note on video and biometric-derived data — [LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]

Runlens computes gait metrics and joint-angle measurements from footage of your
body. Depending on jurisdiction, video of an identifiable person and/or
body-measurement data derived from it **may qualify as sensitive,
special-category, or biometric data** (e.g., GDPR Art. 9; Illinois BIPA; Texas
CUBI; Washington MHMDA and similar state biometric/health statutes), which can
trigger explicit-consent, notice, retention-schedule, and disclosure
obligations.
